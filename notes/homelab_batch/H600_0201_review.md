## Finding
The function qmul is incorrectly implementing quaternion multiplication. The order of multiplication is reversed, which will produce incorrect results when used for transformations.

## Evidence

## Suggested fix
Swap the order of the quaternions in the multiplication. The correct formula for quaternion multiplication is qmul(a, b) = a * b, not b * a.
