## Finding

However, it does not reset other fog properties that may have been modified by the `apply` function, such as `fog_max_opacity` and `fog_cutoff_distance`. This could leave residual fog settings that do not match the 'off' state.

## Evidence

Reset all fog properties to their default values when `off` is called. For example, set `fog_max_opacity` to 0.0 and `fog_cutoff_distance` to 0.0.
