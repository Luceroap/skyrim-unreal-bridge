# review: unreal/SkyBridge/npc_live.py:410 _plan

Local-model review lead; NOT independently verified or applied.

## Finding
The function `_plan` may incorrectly apply the `FLIP` scaling factor to the `tB0` vector when computing the `c` variable. This could lead to incorrect bone positioning in the imported space, especially for bones that are not intended to be mirrored.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/npc_live.py:423 - c = (FLIP[0] * tB0[0] - U * p0[0], FLIP[1] * tB0[1] - U * p0[1], FLIP[2] * tB0[2] - U * p0[2])

## Suggested fix
Remove the `FLIP` scaling factor from the `tB0` vector in the `c` calculation, as it should only be applied to the `p0` vector, not `tB0`. The corrected line should be: `c = (tB0[0] - U * p0[0], tB0[1] - U * p0[1], tB0[2] - U * p0[2])`.

## Validation needed
The developer should run a test with a known bone that is not mirrored (e.g., a leg or arm) and verify that its position in the imported space is correct after the fix.
