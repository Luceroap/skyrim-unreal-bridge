## Finding
The `draw_compass` function may not correctly handle compass labels for directions other than N, E, S, and W. It uses a dictionary to map only these four directions, which could lead to missing or incorrect labels for other cardinal directions.

## Evidence
- remaster_watch.py:172 - label = {0: "N", 90: "E", 180: "S", 270: "W"}.get(deg)

## Suggested fix
Add support for additional cardinal directions (e.g., NE, NW, SE, SW) by expanding the dictionary used to map degrees to labels. For example, add entries for 45, 135, 225, and 315 degrees.

## Validation needed
Verify that the compass now displays labels for all cardinal directions by testing with a variety of heading values. Ensure that the labels are correctly positioned and rendered on the compass.
