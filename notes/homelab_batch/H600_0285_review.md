## Finding
The function `apply_quality` sets the `cast_volumetric_shadow` property of the sun's light component based on `fog_on`, which is derived from the `fog` tuple in the quality tier. However, this logic may be incorrect because `cast_volumetric_shadow` should depend on whether volumetric fog is enabled, not just on `fog_on` being true. This could lead to incorrect shadow behavior when volumetric fog is disabled but `fog_on` is true.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/atmosphere.py:155 - setp(sun, "cast_volumetric_shadow", bool(fog_on))               # god rays through trees and gaps in buildings

## Suggested fix
Change the line to use a more accurate condition, such as whether volumetric fog is enabled, rather than just `fog_on`. For example, use `bool(q['fog'][0])` to ensure that volumetric fog is actually enabled before casting volumetric shadows.

## Validation needed
Test the function with a quality tier where `fog_on` is true but volumetric fog is not enabled, and verify whether `cast_volumetric_shadow` is set correctly.
