# review: unreal/SkyBridge/atmosphere.py:185 look

Local-model review lead; NOT independently verified or applied.

## Finding
The function `look` sets the `directional_inscattering_color` to black when `interior` is True, which may incorrectly suppress sun glow in interior scenes where light scattering should still be visible.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/atmosphere.py:229 - fc.set_directional_inscattering_color(unreal.LinearColor(0, 0, 0, 1))

## Suggested fix
Replace the black color with a non-zero value that represents the desired inscattering color for interior lighting, such as a soft white or warm hue.

## Validation needed
Test the interior lighting with the modified inscattering color to ensure sun glow is visible and visually appropriate.
