## Finding
The function _ensure_rest may fail to correctly read the reference pose of bones if the bone names retrieved from the component are not unique or if the get_bone_transform_by_name method does not return consistent results for the same bone name.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/npc_live.py:381 - names = set(str(comp.get_bone_name(i)) for i in range(n))

## Suggested fix
Replace the use of a set for bone names with a list to ensure that the bone names are processed in a deterministic order and to avoid potential issues with duplicate or inconsistent bone names.

## Validation needed
Verify that the bone names are unique and that the get_bone_transform_by_name method returns consistent results for the same bone name across multiple calls.
