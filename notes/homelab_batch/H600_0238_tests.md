## Finding
The function _is_bind checks if all limb bones are at the same angle from their rest pose, assuming a pure root rotation. However, it may fail to correctly detect when the skeleton is un-animated if the limb bones are not properly aligned or if the quaternions are not normalized.

## Suggested fix
