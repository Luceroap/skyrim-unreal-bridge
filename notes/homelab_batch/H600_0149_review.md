## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/live_bridge.py:618 - a = self.spawn(unreal.PointLight); a.set_folder_path("SkyBridge/Lights")

## Suggested fix
Replace the line `if i >= len(self.light_actors):` with `if i >= len(self.light_actors) or i >= len(self.light_vals):` to ensure that the correct number of light actors is created and updated.

## Validation needed
Verify that the number of light actors matches the number of light values being processed by checking the length of `self.light_actors` and `self.light_vals` after the loop.
