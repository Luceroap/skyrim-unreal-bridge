# tests: unreal/SkyBridge/npc_live.py:393 _ref_scales

Local-model review lead; NOT independently verified or applied.

## Finding
The function _ref_scales computes reference component-space scales for bones in an FBX mesh. It recursively applies parent bone scales to child bones, ensuring that the mesh maintains its intended proportions. However, the logic for composing transforms may not account for all edge cases, such as bones with no parent or incorrect transform composition.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/npc_live.py:405 - cs[nm] = unreal.MathLibrary.compose_transforms(loc, cs[par]) if par in cs else loc

## Suggested fix
Test Case 1: A bone with no parent should have its scale applied directly without any parent transform. Input: A bone with no parent. Expected: The scale is set to the bone's own scale without any parent influence.

Test Case 2: A bone with a parent should have its scale computed as the product of the parent's scale and its own scale. Input: A parent bone with scale (2.0, 2.0, 2.0) and a child bone with scale (1.0, 1.0, 1.0). Expected: The child's scale is (2.0, 2.0, 2.0).

Test Case 3: Transform composition should correctly combine parent and child transforms. Input: A parent transform with rotation and scale, and a child transform with rotation and scale. Expected: The composed transform should reflect the combined rotation and scale of both transforms.

## Validation needed
Mock the Unreal MathLibrary.compose_transforms function to verify that the transform composition is correct. In-game verification would involve checking the visual scale of bones in the FBX mesh after applying the computed scales.
