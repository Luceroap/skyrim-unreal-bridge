# review: tools/distant_world.py:54 read_plugin

## Finding
The function `read_plugin` does not handle decompression correctly for `DATA` records when the `zlib` compression flag is set. This can lead to incorrect parsing of record data, potentially causing malformed or missing entries in the output.

## Evidence
- tools/distant_world.py:69 - if flags & 0x40000: body = zlib.decompress(body[4:])

## Suggested fix
Ensure that the decompression is applied to the entire body of the record, not just a slice of it. The current code slices `body[4:]` before decompression, which may not be correct if the entire body is compressed.

## Validation needed
Test the function with a compressed `DATA` record to confirm that the decompression logic correctly handles the full body of the record.
