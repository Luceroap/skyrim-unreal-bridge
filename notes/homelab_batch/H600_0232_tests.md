## Finding
The function `_ensure_rest` is responsible for reading the reference pose of every bone in a skeletal mesh. It ensures that the rest pose is available before any bone movement occurs. However, the function does not handle cases where the skeletal mesh may not be fully imported or the bone data may be incomplete or inconsistent.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/npc_live.py:373 - Read the reference pose of EVERY bone once, before any bone is moved (a child's component-space transform changes when its parent moves).
