## Finding
The function _smooth uses a hardcoded scale factor 'U' (1.4287) when setting the actor's location, but does not account for the scale of the actor itself. This can lead to incorrect positioning when actors are scaled (e.g., dragons or giants).

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/npc_live.py:15 - U = 1.4287

## Suggested fix
Multiply the scale factor 'U' by the actor's scale before applying it to the location. For example, replace 'U' with 'U * sc' in line 1245.
