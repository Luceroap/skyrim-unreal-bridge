# review: unreal/SkyBridge/weather_fx.py:99 snow_mesh

Local-model review lead; NOT independently verified or applied.

## Finding
The function snow_mesh() assumes that the duplicated asset will have the correct bounds extension, but it does not account for the possibility that the original asset's scale or dimensions may differ significantly from the expected values. This could lead to incorrect bounds and improper rendering of the snow flakes.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/weather_fx.py:105 - ext = unreal.Vector(2.0 * SNOW_BOX / SNOW_SCALE[0], 2.0 * SNOW_BOX / SNOW_SCALE[1], 2.0 * SNOW_HEIGHT / SNOW_SCALE[2])

## Suggested fix
Instead of hardcoding the bounds extension based on SNOW_BOX and SNOW_SCALE, the function should calculate the bounds extension based on the actual dimensions of the duplicated asset. This ensures that the bounds are accurate and consistent with the asset's geometry.

## Validation needed
The developer should verify that the bounds extension is correctly calculated and that the duplicated asset has the expected dimensions. This can be done by inspecting the asset in the Unreal Editor and checking its bounds.
