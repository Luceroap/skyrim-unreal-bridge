Local-model review lead; NOT independently verified or applied.

## Finding
The compass drawing logic may fail to render labels correctly for directions that are not cardinal (N, E, S, W) due to the use of a dictionary that only maps 0, 90, 180, and 270 degrees to labels. This could result in missing or incorrect labels for other directions.

## Evidence
- remaster_watch.py:172 - label = {0: "N", 90: "E", 180: "S", 270: "W"}.get(deg)

## Suggested fix
Update the dictionary to include labels for other directions (e.g., NE, NW, SE, SW) or use a different method to determine labels for all directions.

## Validation needed
Test the compass rendering with headings that are not cardinal (e.g., 45 degrees) to confirm whether labels are displayed correctly.
