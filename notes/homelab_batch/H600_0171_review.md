## Finding

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/live_bridge.py:904 - if str(sm.material_slot_name).lower() in ("null", "none"):

## Suggested fix
Change the condition to check for a case-insensitive match of 'null' or 'none', including potential whitespace, by using `str(sm.material_slot_name).strip().lower() in ('null', 'none')`.

## Validation needed
Verify that material slot names with variations like 'Null ', 'NONE', or 'null none' are correctly identified and replaced with `M_SkyWater`.
