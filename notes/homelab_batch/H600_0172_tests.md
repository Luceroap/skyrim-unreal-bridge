## Finding
The function `water_slots` attempts to replace 'Null' or 'None' material slots with a water material (`M_SkyWater`) for Skyrim water meshes. However, it does not explicitly set the material for the slot, which may leave the slot unchanged if the loop does not find a matching material slot name.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/live_bridge.py:904 - if str(sm.material_slot_name).lower() in ("null", "none"):

## Suggested fix
After identifying a 'Null' or 'None' material slot, explicitly assign the water material to that slot. For example, if the condition is met, set `sm.material = mat` to ensure the material is applied.

## Validation needed
To verify the fix, a developer should run the Unreal Editor with the plugin enabled and observe if water meshes (e.g., canals, pools) are rendered with the correct material (`M_SkyWater`) instead of appearing as empty black beds. Additionally, the developer should check the material slot names and confirm that the water material is applied to the correct slots.
