# review: unreal/SkyBridge/weather_fx.py:229 splash_material

Local-model review lead; NOT independently verified or applied.

## Finding
The splash_material function does not account for the vertical depth of the splash decal, which could lead to incorrect visual effects on surfaces that are not near-horizontal.

## Evidence
- unreal/SkyBridge/weather_fx.py:27 - SPLASH_RADIUS, SPLASH_DEPTH = 1100.0, 450.0  # decal half extents (cm): 22 m across, +-4.5 m vertically around the feet

## Suggested fix
Incorporate the SPLASH_DEPTH parameter into the material graph to ensure the splash decal respects the vertical bounds of the surface it is projected onto.

## Validation needed
Verify that the splash decal is correctly constrained within the vertical bounds defined by SPLASH_DEPTH by testing on surfaces with varying inclinations and heights.
