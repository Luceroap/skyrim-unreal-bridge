## Finding
The function `dump_world` filters references based on a `worldspace` (`ws`) parameter, but it does not handle the case where the `worlds` dictionary may not contain the key `r.get('world')`, leading to a potential `KeyError`.

## Evidence
- tools/distant_world.py:259 - lambda r: worlds.get(r.get("world")) == ws

## Suggested fix
Replace `worlds.get(r.get('world')) == ws` with `worlds.get(r.get('world'), None) == ws` to avoid KeyError when `r.get('world')` is not present in `worlds`.
