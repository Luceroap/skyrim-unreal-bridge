# review: tools/nif2obj.py:53 mat_mul

Local-model review lead; NOT independently verified or applied.

## Finding
The mat_mul function incorrectly computes the translation component of the composed transformation. It does not account for the rotation of the translation vector from the child transformation, leading to incorrect transformation composition.

## Evidence
- tools/nif2obj.py:57 - tt = tuple(ps * sum(pr[i * 3 + k] * t[k] for k in range(3)) + pt[i] for i in range(3))

## Suggested fix
The translation component should be computed as ps * (rot * t) + pt, where rot is the rotation matrix from the child transformation. This ensures that the translation vector is rotated before being scaled and added to the parent's translation.

Test the mat_mul function with known transformation matrices and vectors to verify that the computed translation is correct after applying rotation and scaling.
