## Finding
The function `limb_angles` may produce incorrect angle calculations due to an incorrect use of the trace matrix. The calculation assumes that the trace of the rotation matrix corresponds to the cosine of the angle between the current and rest pose, but this is only valid for a single rotation axis. The formula used does not account for the full rotation and may not correctly represent the angle of the limb.

## Evidence
- tools/debug/walk_check.py:28 - out[raw.split(" [")[0][4:]] = round(math.degrees(math.acos(max(-1, min(1, (rel[0] + rel[4] + rel[8] - 1) / 2)))), 1)

## Suggested fix
Replace the formula for calculating the angle with a more accurate method for determining the angle between two rotation matrices. For example, use the axis-angle representation or the Euler angles to compute the angle of rotation.

## Validation needed
The developer should validate the angle calculation by comparing the computed angles with known correct values for specific limb poses. They should also test the function with different limb configurations to ensure that the calculation is accurate across a range of inputs.
